Privacy Policy

Shorts Pipeline · Google Cloud project shorts-pipeline-509116 · Last updated: 2026-10-07

Shorts Pipeline is a private automation tool that uploads YouTube Shorts on behalf of its owner. It is not a consumer product and is not offered to the public.

Data we access

  • The app requests only the Google OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload so it can upload Shorts via the YouTube Data API.
  • Uploads are performed for the Google account novikovy.ya@gmail.com.

How tokens are stored

  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the machine that runs the pipeline.
  • Tokens are not sold, shared, or otherwise disclosed to third parties.

What we do not collect

  • No end-user analytics.
  • No advertising.
  • No collection of personal data beyond the Google OAuth token needed for upload.

Contact

Questions about this policy: novikovy.ya@gmail.com.
